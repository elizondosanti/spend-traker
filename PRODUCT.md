# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

A person in Costa Rica who spends with a BAC Credomatic card and wants the month's cap to update without typing each receipt. The first user is a student checking the budget on a phone.

## Product Purpose

Spend Traker records BAC purchases, SINPE transfers, and service payments into monthly category caps. Success is a movement that arrives from the bank email and lands in the right category, with a place to confirm anything the app is unsure about.

## Positioning

The receipt enters from the BAC email in Gmail, read only. The account is email and password. Connecting Gmail is not connecting the bank.

## Operating Context

Spanish by default, with an English toggle. Costa Rica time. Amounts in colones and dollars. The person uses Inicio to see the month, Movimientos to add or filter a movement, and Ajustes to connect Gmail.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Reads purchase, SINPE, and service-payment emails. Categories have monthly caps. A movement can be added by hand. Gmail access is read-only. The public app runs on Vercel with Neon. Light and dark mode are both required.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Spend Traker. The brand green is #0F4C5C. Text on that green is white. The logo ink is #1F2937. The button says Conectar Gmail.

## Personality

Quiet ledger confidence — closer to Apple Settings / Moody’s research than to a startup marketing site. Teal chrome, white paper cards, IBM Plex, tabular money as the loudest element. No purple gradients, no glow, no generic Inter look. Motion only for press feedback and occasional panel/form entrance. The product feels like a private monthly ledger for Costa Rica, not a bank login and not a fintech hype deck.

## Evidence on Hand

Real BAC email samples live in the parser tests. Do not invent customers, interviews, or a claim that Gmail has been proven end to end in production.

## Product Principles

- The month's remaining money is the first thing to see.
- A category total must open into the movements that make it.
- The app asks only for what it cannot read from the email.
- Motion explains an opening panel or form, and stays off the rest of the screen.
